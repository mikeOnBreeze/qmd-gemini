# Remote Work Policy and Guidelines

## Working from Home Guidelines

This document outlines our company's approach to remote work, including communication norms, equipment requirements, and team coordination practices.

### Core Principles

1. **Results over presence** — We measure output, not hours online
2. **Default to async** — Write things down instead of scheduling meetings
3. **Intentional connection** — Schedule time for relationship building
4. **Trust and accountability** — Adults managing their own time

## Communication Norms

### Asynchronous Messaging

Most communication should be asynchronous. Write clear, self-contained messages that don't require an immediate response. Use Slack threads, not DMs, for work discussions so context is searchable.

Expected response times:
- Slack: within 4 hours during work hours
- Email: within 24 hours
- Urgent/P0 issues: use phone or Slack with @here

### Video Calls and Camera Etiquette

**When to use video calls**:
- 1:1s and skip-levels
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Difficult or sensitive conversations
- Team social events

**Camera policy**: Cameras on is encouraged but not required. We understand that async messaging and written updates are often more effective than synchronous video. If you're in a meeting, try to be present — don't multitask.

**Meeting hygiene**:
- Always have an agenda
- Start on time, end 5 minutes early
- Record for those who can't attend
- Follow up with written notes and action items

## Security and VPN

### Remote Work VPN Requirements

All remote employees must:

1. Connect to the company VPN when accessing internal tools
2. Use the approved VPN client (currently WireGuard)
3. Never access production systems from public WiFi without VPN
4. Keep VPN client updated to the latest version
5. Report any connection issues to IT immediately

### Device Security

- Enable full-disk encryption (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows)
- Use a password manager for all credentials
- Enable 2FA on all work accounts
- Lock your screen when stepping away
- Don't install unapproved software on work devices

## Team Coordination

### Time Zones

We're distributed across US Pacific, Eastern, and Central European time. Our overlap window is 9am-12pm Pacific / 12pm-3pm Eastern / 6pm-9pm CET.

Schedule meetings during overlap hours only. Respect boundaries — don't send Slack messages expecting responses outside someone's working hours.

### Quarterly Team Gathering

Every quarter, the entire team gathers in person for a week-long offsite. These events include:

- **Planning sessions** — Set goals for the next quarter
- **Hackathon day** — Build something fun together
- **Team dinners and activities** — Bond outside of work
- **Travel** — Company covers flights, hotel, and per diem

The quarterly gathering is mandatory. Submit travel requests at least 3 weeks in advance. We rotate locations each quarter to share the travel burden.

### Daily Standups

Written async standup in the #standup Slack channel by 10am local time:
- What I did yesterday
- What I'm doing today
- Any blockers

## Equipment and Stipend

### Home Office Setup

Every remote employee receives:
- $2,000 one-time home office stipend (desk, chair, monitor)
- $100/month internet stipend
- Company laptop (refreshed every 3 years)
- $500/year learning and development budget

### Ergonomics

Take care of your workspace:
- External monitor at eye level
- Separate keyboard and mouse
- Chair with lumbar support
- Proper lighting for video calls
- Take breaks — follow the 20-20-20 rule
